From the head coach

Why I opened CMG

I grew up in Danville and wrestled at Timberlane. After six years of Division I wrestling at Lehigh and Virginia Tech, I came home and opened CMG in 2026 to give wrestlers in this area a place to train the right way, all year long.

I started the academy with Taylor Donovan, my old teammate at Timberlane, and Kyle Gora from Alvirne. Conor Maslanek and Matt Boucher have joined us since. Between the five of us, we have wrestled at just about every level there is, and we coach every kid who walks in the door, whether it is their first season or they are chasing a state title.

What I learned from my college coaches about technique, managing weight, nutrition, lifting and the mental side of the sport is what we teach here. We want our wrestlers to work hard, shake hands win or lose, and take that discipline with them when they leave the room.

Facility

Inside the academy

We are on the second floor of the Phanzone Sports Center. Everything up here was built for our wrestlers.

Wrestling room

2,185 square feet of Resilite mat. The main section is the mat that was used for the 2026 NCAA Division III national finals, and the room is lined with brand-new Century wall mats so wrestlers can train hard and stay safe.

More mat is on the way to fill out the rest of the room.

Weights and conditioning

A full gym of Rogue weights, plus a conditioning area with an assault bike, ladders, bands and jump ropes.

Locker rooms

Two full-size locker rooms, men's and women's, for academy wrestlers only. Each one has two toilets, two showers and two sinks, plus space to change and store your things during practice.

Parent seating

Parents and family are welcome to stay. There is plenty of seating with a clear view of the mat, so come in, get comfortable and watch your wrestler work.

Gear

We keep a gear store at the academy with CMG shirts, singlets and hoodies. There is also a free filtered cold-water dispenser for wrestlers.

Questions

Good to know

Can my wrestler try a practice before signing up?

Yes. Every wrestler gets one free practice to see if they like it. Send us a message so we know to expect you.

What ages do you coach?

Our main program is for middle school and high school wrestlers. We also run a beginners program for kids ages 7 and up.

Do I still register online if I am paying in person?

Yes. Sign up for the membership you want on our registration page and choose the pay later option when you get to payment. Then bring cash, a check or Venmo to the front desk and we will mark you as paid.

Can parents stay and watch?

Absolutely. We have a seating area with a view of the mat and we like having parents in the room.

Are you open in the off-season?

Yes. We run practice five days a week, year-round.

How do I know when my membership is ending?

We will let you know seven days before it runs out so you can decide whether to renew or take a break. You can also check your membership any time in the Gymdesk app.
